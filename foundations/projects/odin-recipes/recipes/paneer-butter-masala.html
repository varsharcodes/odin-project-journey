• Paneer Butter Masala

    Serves : 2-3

    Time : 30 minutes

    Ingredients :

    • 250 g paneer, cubed
    • 2 medium tomatoes
    • 1 medium onion
    • 1 tbsp butter
    • 1 tsp ginger-garlic paste
    • 1/2 tsp turmeric
    • 1 tsp coriander powder
    • 1/2 tsp garam masala
    • 1/2 tsp cumin
    • 2 tbsp cream
    • 1/2 cup water
    • Salt to taste
    • Coriander for garnish

    Method :

    1. Heat 1 tbsp oil and lightly fry the paneer cubes until golden. Remove and keep aside.
    2. In the same pan, add cumin and onion. Cook until the onion becomes translucent.
    3. Add chopped tomatoes and cook until they become soft.
    4. Add chopped tomatoes and cook until they become soft.
    5. Let the mixture cool slightly, then blend it into a smooth paste.
    6. Heat butter in the pan and add the blended mixture.
    7. Add turmeric, chilli powder, coriander powder, salt, and ½ cup water.
    8. Simmer for 7–8 minutes until the gravy thickens.
    9. Add paneer and cook for another 3–4 minutes.
    10. Add garam masala and cream. Mix gently.
    11. Garnish with coriander.
