Moong Dal Chilla

Makes : 6-8 chillas

Time : 30-40 minutes

Ingredients :

  • 1 cup yellow or green moong dal
  • 1 small onion, finely chopped
  • 1 small tomato, finely chopped
  • 1 green chilli, finely chopped
  • 1 tsp grated ginger
  • 2 tbsp coriander, chopped
  • 1/2 tsp cumin
  • 1/4 tsp turmeric
  • 1/2 tsp red chilli powder
  • Salt to taste
  • Water as needed
  • Oil or ghee for cooking

Optional Filling

  • Grated paneer
  • Grated cheese
  • Finely chopped vegetables

Method :

  1. Wash the moong dal thoroughly.
  2. Soak the moong dal in plenty of water for 3-4 hours.
  3. Drain the water completely.
  4. Add the soaked dal to a blender.
  5. Add a small amount of water and blend into a smooth, slightly thick batter.
  6. Transfer the batter to a bowl.
  7. Add onion, tomato, green chilli, ginger, coriander, cumin, turmeric, red chilli powder, and salt.
  8. Mix everything well.
  9. Heat a non-stick or well-seasoned iron pan.
  10. Lightly grease the pan with oil or ghee.
  11. Pour approximately 1/4 cup batter onto the center of the pan.
  12. Spread the batter outward in a circular motion to make a thin pancake.
  13. Drizzle a little oil around the edges.
  14. Cook for about 2 minutes until the bottom becomes golden.
  15. Flip the chilla carefully.
  16. Cook the other side for about 1 minute.
  17. Remove from the pan and repeat with the remaining batter.

For the Paneer Filling

  1. Spread the chilla batter on the hot pan.
  2. Sprinkle grated paneer over the surface.
  3. Add a little black pepper and chaat masala.
  4. Fold the chilla in half.
  5. Cook for another minute.

Tip: If the chilla breaks while flipping, the batter may be too thin. Add a little rice flour or besan to make the batter slightly thicker.
