Vegetable Spring Rolls

Serves : 3-4

Time : 40-45 minutes

Ingredients :

  • For the Filling

    • 1 cup finely shredded cabbage
    • 1/2 cup grated carrot
    • 1/2 cup thinly sliced capsicum
    • 1/2 cup finely chopped spring onions
    • 1/2 cup beans chopped spring onions
    • 2-3 cloves garlic, finely chopped
    • 1 tbsp grated ginger
    • 1 tbsp soy sauce
    • 1 tsp vinegar
    • 1/2 tsp black pepper
    • 1/2 tsp salt, or to taste
    • 1 tsp sesame oil or regular cooking oil
    • 1 tsp cornflour mixed with 2 tbsp water

  • For the Rolls

    • 8-10 spring-roll wrappers
    • 2 tbsp flour + 3 tbsp water, mixed into a paste
    • Oil for frying

Method :

  1. Heat 1 tbsp oil in a work or larger pan on high heat.
  2. Add garlic and ginger. Stir-fry for about 20-30 seconds.
  3. Add carrots, beans, cabbage, and capsicum.
  4. Stir-fry on high heat for 2-3 minutes.Keep the vegetables slightly crunchy.
  5. Add soy sauce, vinegar, black pepper, and salt.
  6. Mix well and cook for another minute.
  7. Add the cornflour slurry and stir continously for 30-40 seconds.
  8. Turn off the heat and let the filling cool completely
  9. Place one wrapper in front of you like a diamond.
  10. Put about 2 tbsp filling near the bottom-center.
  11. Fold the bottom corner over the filling.
  12. Fold both side corners inward.
  13. Roll it tightly upward.
  14. Seal the final edge with the flour-water paste.
  15. Repeat with the remaining wrappers.
  16. Heat oil on medium heat.
  17. Fry the spring rolls until golden and cripsy.
  18. Drain on a wire rack or paper towel.

Tip: Don't overfill the wrappers, and make sure the filling is cool and relatively dry. This prevents the rolls from becoming soggy or bursting.
