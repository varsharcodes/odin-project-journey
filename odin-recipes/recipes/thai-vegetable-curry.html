Thai Vegetable Curry

Serves : 3-4

Time : 30-35 minutes

Ingredients :

  • For the Vegetables

    • 1 cup broccoli
    • 1 cup bell peppers
    • 1 cup carrots
    • 1 cup mushrooms
    • 1/2 cup baby corn
    • 1/2 cup zucchini
    • 1/2 cup tofu, cubed

  • For the Curry

    • 1 tbsp oil
    • 2-3 tbsp vegetarian Thai curry paste
    • 400 ml coconut milk
    • 1/2 cup water or vegetable stock
    • 1 tbsp soy sauce
    • 1 tsp brown sugar
    • 1 tsp lime juice
    • Salt to taste

  • For Garnishing

    • Fresh basil
    • Fresh coriander
    • Red chilli
    • Spring onions

Method :

  1. Cut all the vegetables into bite-sized pieces.
  2. Keep harder vegetables such as carrots and broccoli separate from softer vegetables such as mushrooms and zucchini.
  3. Heat 1 tbsp oil in a large pan.
  4. Add the vegetarian Thai curry paste.
  5. Fry the curry paste for 1-2 minutes until fragrant.
  6. Add about 1/2 cup coconut milk.
  7. Stir well and cook for about 2 minutes.
  8. Add carrots, broccoli, and baby corn.
  9. Cook for about 3-4 minutes.
  10. Add mushrooms, bell peppers, zucchini, and tofu.
  11. Add the remaining coconut milk.
  12. Add 1/2 cup water or vegetable stock.
  13. Add soy sauce and brown sugar.
  14. Simmer gently for 5-8 minutes.
  15. Check the vegetables. They should be cooked but still have some bite.
  16. Add lime juice.
  17. Taste and adjust the salt and other seasonings.
  18. Garnish with fresh basil, coriander, red chilli, or spring onions.
  19. Serve hot with steamed jasmine rice.

Tip: Check that the Thai curry paste is vegetarian because some Thai curry pastes contain shrimp paste or fish sauce. Do not boil the coconut milk aggressively; keep the curry at a gentle simmer for a smoother texture.
