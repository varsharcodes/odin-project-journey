Pav Bhaji

Serves : 4

Time : 45-50 minutes

Ingredients :

  • For the Bhaji

    • 3 medium potatoes
    • 1 cup cauliflower
    • 1/2 cup green peas
    • 1 medium carrot
    • 1 medium capsicum
    • 2 medium tomatoes
    • 1 large onion
    • 2 tbsp pav bhaji masala
    • 1 tsp red chilli powder
    • 1/2 tsp turmeric
    • 1 tsp coriander powder
    • 1/2 tsp cumin seeds
    • Salt to taste
    • 2-3 tbsp butter
    • 1 tbsp oil
    • 1 tsp ginger-garlic paste
    • 1 tbsp lemon juice
    • Fresh coriander, chopped

  • For the Pav

    • 8 pav
    • 1-2 tbsp butter
    • 1/2 tsp pav bhaji masala

Method :

  1. Chop the potatoes, cauliflower, carrot, and capsicum into small pieces.
  2. Add the chopped vegetables and green peas to a pressure cooker.
  3. Add about 1 1/2 cups water and a little salt.
  4. Pressure cook until the vegetables become very soft.
  5. Mash the cooked vegetables thoroughly using a potato masher.
  6. Heat 1 tbsp oil and 1 tbsp butter in a large pan.
  7. Add cumin seeds and let them crackle.
  8. Add finely chopped onions and cook until they become translucent.
  9. Add ginger-garlic paste and cook for about 1 minute.
  10. Add finely chopped tomatoes and cook until they become soft.
  11. Add turmeric, red chilli powder, coriander powder, and pav bhaji masala.
  12. Mix well and cook for 1-2 minutes.
  13. Add the mashed vegetables to the pan.
  14. Add water gradually until the bhaji reaches a thick but pourable consistency.
  15. Mash everything together and mix well.
  16. Cook on low-medium heat for 10-15 minutes, stirring occasionally.
  17. Add another tablespoon of butter.
  18. Add lemon juice and adjust the salt and spices according to taste.
  19. Garnish with freshly chopped coriander.
  20. Cut the pav horizontally.
  21. Heat a pan and add a little butter.
  22. Sprinkle a little pav bhaji masala over the butter.
  23. Place the pav cut-side down and toast until lightly crisp and golden.

Tip: Mash the vegetables thoroughly to get the smooth texture that pav bhaji is known for. Serve with chopped onion, coriander, lemon wedges, and buttered pav.
